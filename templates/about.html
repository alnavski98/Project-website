ABOUT ME

Electronics engineer focused on practical problem solving.

I work at the intersection of electronics, embedded systems and hardware testing. I am particularly interested in work where technical analysis is combined with hands-on testing, measurement and development.

BACKGROUND

Engineering through testing and understanding

I have a background in electronics engineering and industrial cybernetics, with experience involving electronic hardware, embedded development, testing and troubleshooting.

I prefer to understand systems in detail rather than treating individual problems in isolation. This means examining how hardware, firmware, interfaces, power, measurements and operating conditions affect one another.

My approach is systematic and practical. I value clear requirements, suitable test equipment, documented observations and test procedures that make results reproducible.

HOW I WORK

My engineering approach

01

Understand the system

I begin by identifying requirements, interfaces, operating conditions and expected behaviour.

02

Measure before assuming

I use measurements and observations to separate actual behaviour from assumptions.

03

Isolate the cause

I break complex problems into smaller testable parts to identify the underlying cause.

04

Document the result

I document procedures, findings and decisions so that the work can be repeated and improved.

MOTIVATION

What I look for in technical work

I am motivated by roles where I can take ownership of technical problems, investigate systems thoroughly and contribute to practical improvements.

I work best when there is a short path between identifying a technical need and being able to test it. Access to suitable tools, components and equipment makes it possible to evaluate ideas efficiently and base decisions on measurable results.

I also value cooperation between hardware, firmware and software disciplines, particularly when responsibilities and technical decisions are communicated clearly.
